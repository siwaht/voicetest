The Office Day Out

The morning sun filtered through the glass doors as Sarah, Marcus, and Lisa arrived at the office building together. They greeted each other with warm smiles in the elevator, ready for another productive day.

After a long day of meetings and project work, the three colleagues decided to unwind at their favorite restaurant downtown. The hostess led them to a cozy corner table by the window.

Sarah settled into her seat and ordered a grilled salmon with roasted vegetables. "I need something light after all those presentations today," she said, stretching her shoulders.

Marcus scanned the menu and grinned. "Give me the biggest burger you've got, with extra fries. I'm starving!" He laughed and leaned back in his chair.

Lisa chose the vegetarian pasta primavera. "The fresh ingredients here are amazing," she said enthusiastically. "Plus, it's comfort food without feeling too heavy."

As their meals arrived, the conversation flowed easily. Marcus talked about his new project proposal, while Lisa shared a funny story about the printer jamming during the morning standup. Sarah listened and smiled, occasionally adding her own observations about the day.

Between bites of their meals, they discussed weekend plans and laughed about office mishaps. The stress of the workday seemed to melt away with each shared moment.

As they finished their meals and ordered coffee, they all agreed it was the perfect way to end the day. Tired but satisfied, they prepared to head home, grateful for both good work and good company.
